Vépárpup - Watertongue

Lore

Vépárpup is a conlang that I am creating for the Pokémon Vaporeon.
This is part of a Pokémon headcanon that I have where Pokémon in general just have our own languages.
I'll leave a loredump about this headcanon below if you feel like reading my ramblings :P

phonetic inventory

vépárpup has 10 consonants and 10 vowels
the consonants (followed by the IPA symbol for what they represent in brackets) are:

v [v], w [ɰʷ], r [ɾ] or [ɹ]*, n [n], ñ[ɲ], p [p], h [h], j [ç], ' [ʔ], ř [ɽ͡r̥], ŵ [ʋ] and x [xʷ].

the vowels are:
a(a), e(ɛ), i(i), o(ɑ), u(ə), á(ʌ:), é(e:), í(ɨ:), ó(ɤ:) and ú(ɯ:).

*r is [ɹ] when word-initial or when next to a n or ñ.
