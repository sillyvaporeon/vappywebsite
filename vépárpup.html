Vépárpup - Watertongue

Lore

Vépárpup is a conlang that I am creating for the Pokémon Vaporeon.
This is part of a Pokémon headcanon that I have where Pokémon in general just have our own languages.
I'll leave a loredump about this headcanon below if you feel like reading my ramblings :P

Open Loredump

This headcanon is based off of how Pokémon repeat our names to speak, whilst also being able to communicate with each other.
A while into developing Vépárpup, I retroactively started wondering how that would be the case, especially factoring other outliers such as certain Pokémon who can speak human languages like Team Rocket's Meowth in the anime.
I then came up with a concept of Pokémon species sharing languages similarly to moves; species slash evolution trees would be able to learn a language instinctively, like it was built into their DNA.
How general Pokéspeak might work could just be due to some supernatural link between Pokémon species, due to Pokémon having a shared ancestor in Mew.

I think that:
Pokémon naturally pick up on the language of their species / evolution family
They don't have to be around other members, but it makes them pick it up faster
Pokémon can learn other species languages with about the same average difficulty that it takes a human to learn another human language
Pokémon and humans can learn each other's languages but it's very difficult
